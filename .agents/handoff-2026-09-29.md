## Results so far

Same pipeline for every row (CT2 int8, beam 4, no-repeat 3). chrF++ unless
noted; CIs are paired bootstrap 95% from `mt-training compare`.

| Eval | Untuned NLLB-600M | June model | **v1.0.0 model** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FLORES+ devtest (1,012), BLEU / chrF++ | 3.08 / 20.97 | 2.76 / 20.96 | **3.33 / 22.56** |
| Bouquet sentences (854), BLEU / chrF++ | 9.18 / 26.09 | 8.33 / 27.12 | **10.56 / 28.71** |
| Bouquet paragraphs (198), BLEU / chrF++ | 7.49 / 25.34 | 5.92 / 23.63 | **12.46 / 33.77** |
| `burkimbia/mt-benchmark-public` leaderboard, BLEU / **chrF** | 5.22 / 35.10 | – | **7.92 / 42.68** |
| v1.0.0 in-domain test (2,573), BLEU / chrF++ | – | – | 9.88 / 32.19 |

- **v1.0.0 model vs untuned:** FLORES+ +1.78 [+1.40, +2.16], Bouquet
  sentences +2.62, paragraphs +8.42. Gains grow with input length: the older
  models drop content on long inputs (output/reference length 0.59–0.71 on
  Bouquet paragraphs vs 0.94).
- **Leaderboard gain (+7.6 chrF) is inflated.** 147 benchmark sentences of
  6+ words (administrative, health) come from documents in our training data
  (`conseils`, `kade`, `sida`). The transferable gain is closer to
  +2 chrF++.
- **What limits FLORES+ is domain, not length:** every length quartile sits
  at 22–23 chrF++; 73% of long training rows are `conseils`.

## Gotchas learned

- **NLLB tokenizer ignores `src_lang`/`tgt_lang` passed to `__call__`**, no
  warning: set them on the tokenizer.
- **Modal:** `--detach` alone doesn't stop Ctrl+C from cancelling a waiting
  call; use `--detach --no-wait`. Only secrets listed in `secrets=[…]` are
  injected (`huggingface-secret`, `wandb-secret`).
- **After `--resume_from_checkpoint`, the auto model-card results table is
  scrambled** (column shift); read W&B or `trainer_state.json`.
- **4 GB RTX 3050:** smoke tests only (`--model_dtype bfloat16 --optim sgd
  --gradient_checkpointing --max_length 64`); CT2 inference fits well
  (FLORES+ in ~1.5 min).
- **Compare scores within one device and one metric:** int8 CT2 differs
  slightly between GPUs; old `exp_res.md` numbers are plain chrF; the
  leaderboard is plain chrF.
- **`madoss` pushes bypass the "PR required" rule on `moore-web` main.**
